Marginal-action planner · actual deployment vs full target

Enter the asset's total pool and how much is already deployed—whether old or newly contributed. Below full policy, the controller buys one-third of the remaining full-policy shortfall and reassesses after the next weekly close, capped at 10% of the total pool per week. Raw remains a structural posterior reference rather than a stopping target. At full it holds; above full it stops adding but does not force a sale. Top/distribution has priority and is the only layer allowed to trim. Minimum ticket defaults to $0 and the no-trade band defaults to 1.0pp. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Policy history (daily close snapshots)

What this is for: track how the model posterior changes across true close snapshots: closing price, target allocation, calibrated median future low, timing buckets, downside probability and evidence quality. Timing buckets are mutually exclusive, uncalibrated mass from the raw timing center: at/before as-of, next 1–14d, days 15–60 and after 60d. They bypass the rolling 14d publication floor and do not affect policy sizing. Older rows without stored timing sigma retain their legacy timing label until causally reconstructed. Click the date field, jump by year/month, then pick a start and end date (or use a quick preset — 7d / 30d / 90d / 1y / All); defaults to the latest 7 calendar days.
